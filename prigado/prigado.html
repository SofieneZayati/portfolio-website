Project Overview

Problem

Product listing, sales analysis, campaign preparation, and customer risk checks were manual and time consuming, leading to inconsistent data and slow decisions.

Solution

Built AI assistants in n8n that route natural language requests to MySQL workflows, enriching product data, generating marketing assets, and producing analytics on demand.

Impact

Automated key business processes, improved data consistency, and delivered fast, readable outputs for sales, marketing, and logistics teams.

Core Modules

Product Creation Assistant

Multi-path workflow (add product, query product, general help) with price enrichment, category suggestion, tag generation, and MySQL insertion.

Prigado Boost

Marketing and sales automation including email campaigns, sales reports by seller, bundle suggestions, and social post generation.

Prigado Logistics

Predicts activity peaks, ranks trending products, and calculates customer risk using order history and delivery status.

AI Router and JSON Guardrails

Strict intent routing with sanitized JSON outputs, clear handling for empty results, and consistent payloads across branches.

SQL Aggregation for AI

Aggregate nodes consolidate SQL rows into structured arrays, ensuring agents receive clean, readable inputs.

Business Ready Outputs

Reports, recommendations, and campaign content are delivered in formats ready for decision makers and operators.
